# Awesome Clipping [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of clipping platforms, campaign finders, editing tools and guides for paid clipping and UGC campaigns.

Clipping is cutting short clips from a creator's or brand's long videos and posting them on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and X. Brands and creators run campaigns on clipping platforms that pay clippers for the views their posts earn, usually a rate per 1,000 views from a set budget.

## Clipping platforms

Marketplaces where brands and creators post paid campaigns and clippers join them.

- [Clipping.io](https://www.clipping.io) - Clipping service where creators clip viral content and post it for pay.
- [Clipstake](https://www.clipstake.com) - Pay-per-view marketplace connecting brands with creators.
- [Clipster](https://www.clipster.com) - Campaign platform for brands and music, with tracking and automatic creator payouts.
- [Content Rewards](https://contentrewards.com) - Clipping and UGC campaign platform where brands pay creators per 1,000 views, also on [Whop](https://whop.com/discover/content-rewards/).
- [Evangelist](https://evangelist.so) - Clipping and UGC platform for media buyers, run by the same team as ClippingAlpha.
- [Promote.fun](https://www.promote.fun) - Creators post content and get paid for performance.
- [Vyro](https://vyro.com) - Clipping platform where creators get paid per view to post for creators and brands.

## Campaign finders and analytics

Sites that track campaigns across clipping platforms.

- [ClippingAlpha](https://clippingalpha.com) - Ranks live campaigns from several clipping platforms by its own Alpha Score, with pay per 1,000 views, budget left and briefs. Free to browse. Maintained by the authors of this list.
- [ClippingAlpha MCP server](https://github.com/ClippingAlpha/clippingalpha-mcp) - The same campaign data for AI assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor.

## AI clipping tools

Tools that cut long videos into short clips automatically.

- [Klap](https://klap.app) - Turns long videos, podcasts and webinars into TikToks, Reels and Shorts with captions and reframing.
- [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro) - Turns long videos into short clips and publishes them to social platforms.
- [Submagic](https://www.submagic.co) - AI captions, b-roll and edits for short-form videos.
- [Vizard](https://vizard.ai) - Turns long-form videos into short clips with AI.

## Video editors

- [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com) - Video editor with templates and AI tools, on desktop, mobile and web.
- [DaVinci Resolve](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve) - Editing, color, effects and audio in one application, with a free version.
- [Descript](https://www.descript.com) - Video and audio editor where you edit the transcript to edit the video.
- [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com) - Online video editor.
- [Kdenlive](https://kdenlive.org) - Free and open source video editor.
- [Shotcut](https://www.shotcut.org) - Free, open source, cross-platform video editor.
- [VEED](https://www.veed.io) - Online video editor with subtitles, dubbing and AI editing.

## Utilities

- [Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org) - Free, open source audio editor and recorder.
- [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org) - Command line toolkit to convert, cut and process video and audio.
- [HandBrake](https://handbrake.fr) - Open source video transcoder.
- [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com) - Free, open source software for recording and live streaming.
- [Whisper](https://github.com/openai/whisper) - Open source speech recognition model, useful for transcripts and captions.
