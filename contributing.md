# Contribution guidelines

Thanks for helping. A good addition is something a clipper or a brand running clipping campaigns would actually use.

## Adding an entry

- One pull request per entry, with the reason it belongs in the title or description.
- Check the link works and goes to the official page.
- Use this format: `- [Name](https://link) - Short neutral description.`
- Describe what it is in one line, ending with a period. No marketing claims, no pay or income figures, no "best".
- Keep each section in alphabetical order.
- The entry must be live and publicly available. Paid tools are fine.
- Communities must be public and active.

## Not accepted

- Referral or affiliate links.
- Pages that promise income or earnings.
- Bot, view-buying or engagement-buying services.
- Tools that download or repost other people's content against a platform's terms.

## Changes and removals

Open an issue or a pull request if a link is dead, a description is wrong or a service has closed.

By contributing you agree to release your contribution under [CC0](LICENSE).
